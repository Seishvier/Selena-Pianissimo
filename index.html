STORY

THE RISING STAR

Born in Babylonia and raised with aristocrat-style education, Selena became the most anticipated opera singer of the World Government Association of Art. Her voice and musical talent were unmatched — a beacon of culture in a crumbling world.

THE SPACE STATION

During a battle at a space station, Selena went missing. She was rescued by an "Ascendant" and her body was restructured by the Red Tide. The Hetero-core shard inside her nearly turned her into an abomination — but her mind held on.

VOICE OF THE VIRUS

Selena gained the ability to "listen" to the Punishing Virus — to hear its "exotic song" of emotions. She became a Transcendant, wielding sound itself as a weapon. Her cello blade sings for everyone she encounters.

PIANISSIMO

With the Commandant, she chose the name "Pianissimo" — a reminder that the hall of art welcomes everyone. Her new frame was co-developed by the Science Council and WGAA. Though phantom pain remains, her pursuit of beauty is unshaken.

THE IRIS THAT BLOOMS

Now fighting alongside the Commandant and Gray Raven, Selena's melody weaves through every battle. Her voice is both shield and sword — a quiet defiance against the silence of extinction.
